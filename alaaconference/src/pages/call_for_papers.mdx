    Navigating Tradition and Change: How can we incorporate contemporary challenges into legal education and scholarship?
</div>

We are pleased to invite you to the University of Queensland for the **2025 Australasian Law Academics Association (ALAA) Conference**, to be held from Wednesday 2 July to Friday 4 July 2025. We invite legal academics, practitioners, and policymakers to engage in discussions on the evolving landscape of legal education and research. This year’s theme highlights the practical, intellectual, and emotional aspects of balancing contemporary values, priorities, and imperatives in our work.

Legal educators and scholars today are juggling competing demands. Traditional constraints on legal education (regulatory, economic, structural) often exist in tension with the urgent need to incorporate contemporary issues, such as First Nations truth-telling, climate justice, technological transformation, and global debates on equity and inclusion. The evolving legal profession is requiring us to rethink how we teach, research, and prepare students for practice. Legal scholars experiencing increasing resourcing constrains need to rapidly respond to the changing local and global political climate.

The ALAA 2025 Conference seeks to foster dialogue and innovative solutions for these dynamic challenges across **all areas of law**. We encourage submissions that explore:
* Incorporating First Nations perspectives into legal curricula and research: best practices and challenges;
* Pacific perspectives and practices: Exploring contemporary challenges of legal research and education across the Pacific;
* Climate change and environmental justice: integrating discussions of the climate crisis into legal education and scholarship, particularly into the core curriculum;
* Artificial intelligence (AI) and digital technologies: their impact on legal research, assessment, and professional roles and competencies;
* Legal pedagogy and curriculum design: navigating innovation within regulatory constraints;
* Challenges to rule of law: Examining global perspectives on rule of law and judicial independence; and
* Social Justice-Oriented Education: How can we develop an ethical and social-justice-oriented legal education curriculum that instils these values into students?

We welcome individual papers and panel and workshop proposals. 

Please submit your proposal [**here**](https://bit.ly/ALAA2025) by **4 April 2025**.

We look forward to welcoming you to Brisbane in July!

Please direct any queries to the Organising Committee at alaa2025conference@uq.edu.au
